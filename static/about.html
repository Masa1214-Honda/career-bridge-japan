About Career Bridge Japan

We help international talent explore realistic career opportunities with employers in Japan.

Our approach

We begin with a focused profile covering work authorization, job goals, timing, experience, language ability, and CV readiness. This helps our team spend time on relevant, actionable matches.

For candidates

The initial application is free and takes only a few minutes. If your profile matches an active opportunity, our team will contact you for a short conversation.
